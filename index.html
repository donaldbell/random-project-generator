How it works

Hit Roll to spin every bucket ticked Roll. Lock the picks you like and re-roll the rest, or choose any bucket by hand. The difficulty dial filters ingredients and sets the build stage, from a dev board on a breadboard up to a bare module on a custom PCB with an enclosure.

Ingredients carry simple compatibility tags, so a camera only comes up with a brain that can drive one, and a solenoid gets flagged if your power source can't handle it.
